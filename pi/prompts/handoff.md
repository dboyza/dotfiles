---
description: Produce a concise handoff for continuing the work in another session
argument-hint: "[focus]"
---
Prepare a concise but complete handoff for another coding-agent session.
Use `$ARGUMENTS` as additional focus.

Include the original goal, important decisions, files changed, current repository state, validation already performed, unresolved issues, and exact next steps.
Record commands or error messages that the next session would otherwise need to rediscover.
Separate verified facts from assumptions.
Do not modify files, commit, or push while preparing the handoff.
